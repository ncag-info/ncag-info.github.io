Moving servers

August 15, 2016

I just moved everything over to GitHub Pages, and reconfigured the DNS so that instead of a redirect to a subdirectory on the department's server the website is fully functional on the actual domain. We are happy to announce this website, dedicated to news that is of interest to the noncommutative algebraic geometry community. Hopefully it will be of use to you. At the moment it is mostly a list of conferences.
